Why it matters

Evidence, not noise.

International shipping’s greenhouse-gas emissions reached 1,076 million tonnes CO₂e in 2018, according to the IMO’s Fourth GHG Study. Fuel use remains a central operational and climate issue across global transport.

International Maritime Organization · Fourth GHG Study

For ships above 5,000 gross tonnes calling at EU ports, FuelEU Maritime began applying in 2025. It targets a 2% reduction in the annual greenhouse-gas intensity of onboard energy versus 2020, with increasingly stringent targets through 2050.

European Commission · FuelEU Maritime

On the land side, the U.S. EPA says a typical long-haul combination truck that eliminates unnecessary idling can save more than 900 gallons of fuel per year. The exact opportunity varies by route, climate and operating pattern.

U.S. EPA SmartWay · Idle reduction

Research references are sector context, not proof of product-specific performance. FuelSaver 4 Diesel performance should be assessed through a controlled, documented trial in the relevant operation.
